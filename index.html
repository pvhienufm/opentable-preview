iii.The Founder
On the ground

A US national, based in Vietnam for more than a decade. Fluent in Vietnamese.

Most companies don’t fail in Vietnam because it’s hard. They fail because they walked in blind.

I’ve spent over ten years here — in insurance and international client support — watching good companies make avoidable mistakes: the wrong first partner, the wrong sequence, the wrong assumption about how relationships actually work in-market.

Open Table exists so you don’t start from zero. I bring local judgement, a vetted bench of partners, and a method — so you arrive with the right people already at the table, not a stack of introductions you have to vet yourself.

APAC is not one market. I won’t pretend to have offices everywhere. What I offer is narrower and more useful: I know who’s worth trusting, and I’ll only seat them next to you once they’ve earned it.

Hunter Jorden
Founder · Open Table
